Source: instructions supplied by the user for this repository.

- The current notes cover Module III only. React.js and Node.js are excluded, including their listed subtopics.
- Questions from the remaining Module III topics will be mostly theoretical. Keep minimal, useful syntax and examples; do not interpret this as a guarantee of no code questions.
- The syllabus defines the examinable scope, subject to the exclusions above.
- Previous-year questions (PYQs) indicate emphasis, not the complete set of possible questions. Cover every included syllabus subtopic even if no PYQ asks about it.
- The supplied XML, AJAX, jQuery, and JSON PDFs are references. Do not reproduce everything in them or let their omissions narrow the syllabus.

## Depth and writing standard

The notes must support both quick revision and complete, well-structured exam answers. Explain what a concept means, why it is needed, and how it works at the depth the topic requires.

- Use simple, natural English and complete, meaningful sentences. Explain concepts rather than relying on memorized textbook definitions.
- Use large, descriptive headings for major topics and smaller headings for important concepts.
- Prefer short paragraphs for explanations and bullets for properties, steps, and comparisons. Avoid fragmented lists.
- Introduce technical terms naturally and explain them on first use.
- Include clear comparisons and simple, relevant examples where they improve understanding.
- Remove repetition, unnecessary jargon, academic language, and excess detail. Do not shorten notes by compressing useful explanations into unclear phrases.
- Organize material so that a student can understand it quickly and reproduce an answer in the examination.

Quality target: notes written like an excellent teacher's focused explanation, not a summary of lecture slides.
