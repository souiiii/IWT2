## Depth and writing standard

The eventual notes must support both quick revision and complete, well-structured exam answers. Explain what a concept means, why it is needed, and how it works at the depth the topic requires.

- Use simple, natural English and complete, meaningful sentences. Explain concepts rather than relying on memorized textbook definitions.
- Use large, descriptive headings for major topics and smaller headings for important concepts.
- Prefer short paragraphs for explanations and bullets for properties, steps, and comparisons. Avoid fragmented lists.
- Introduce technical terms naturally and explain them on first use.
- Include clear comparisons and simple, relevant examples where they improve understanding.
- Remove repetition, unnecessary jargon, academic language, and excess detail. Do not shorten notes by compressing useful explanations into unclear phrases.
- Organize material so that a student can understand it quickly and reproduce an answer in the examination.

Quality target: notes written like an excellent teacher's focused explanation, not a summary of lecture slides.
